My Story

Sugam Dhungana

I am a passionate Geomatics Engineering student at Kathmandu University with a deep fascination for how spatial data can tell stories about our world. Growing up in Bhaktapur — a UNESCO World Heritage city — gave me an early appreciation for the relationship between place, culture, and geography.

My academic journey began at Sainik Awasiya Mahavidyalaya, Sallaghari, where discipline and rigour shaped my approach to problem-solving. Now at KU, I focus on the intersection of traditional surveying, remote sensing, and modern web technologies to build tools that make geospatial data accessible to everyone.

Outside the lab, I enjoy hiking Nepal's mountain trails, competing in geography quiz events, and contributing to open-source GIS projects. I believe maps are one of the most powerful forms of communication — and I want to spend my career making better ones.
